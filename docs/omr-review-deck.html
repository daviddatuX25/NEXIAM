Core Architectural Axiom
Authority = Frame-Anchored Solver Grid ∧ Signed HMAC-SHA256 Instances

Traditional OMR tools fail because coordinates are measured from the edge of physical paper—which shifts in printer trays and gets distorted when photographed on phones. NEXIAM anchors all coordinates to the optical centers of 4 asymmetric corner fiducials, making mobile scans invariant to rotation, skew, and print displacement.

Tolerance Engineering

Theorem 1.3: Graphite Smear Immunity

Physical limits preventing adjacent bubble fill marks from triggering false multi-marks.

Theorem 1.3 Physical Lower Bounds
Bubble Diameter ≥ 4.2 mm (≈ 50 px) ∧ Option Edge Gap ≥ 1.26 mm (≈ 15 px)

Standard pencil stroke variance on porous 70–80 gsm bond paper has a spread σ ≤ 0.45mm. Enforcing an edge-to-edge gap ≥ 1.26mm guarantees the probability of optical mark bleed between options A and B is P(Bleed) < 10⁻⁵.
