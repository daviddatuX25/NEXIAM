Operational Breakdown

What We Replace & Eliminate

Removing manual friction points from your campus admission pipeline
  • No single-day release crowd rush
  • No manual score re-typing errors
  • No double-booking or overbooked rooms

Core System Architecture

What NEXIAM Is

One shared digital record carrying applicants from application to enrollment
  • Replaces paper files with one shared digital record
  • Gives every office the exact data it needs
  • Keeps consultation at the heart of assessment
Journey Step 1

Apply & Get Accepted

Simple online student application and single-queue review
  • Applicants submit data, grades, and preferences online
  • Registrar reviews, accepts, or corrects from one queue
  • Private access key + birthdate sign-in for security
  • Digital grades stored alongside Form 138/TOR
Journey Step 2

Exam Day, Without the Chaos

Automated capacity limits and flexible session booking
  • Venues picked from room list with built-in capacity
  • System blocks double-booking automatically
  • Staff assign seats or open online self-booking
  • Live proctor roster tracks attendance on exam day
Journey Step 3

Fair, Fast Scoring

Rapid bubble-sheet scanning with human-in-the-loop review
  • Exam booklet stays paper; students fill bubble answer sheets
  • Sheets are scanned — marks read automatically from the scan images
  • Paper conversion table is digitized once — encrypted, confidential, staff-only
  • Raw counts become final scores through that secure digital table
  • Missing scores block approval; a person confirms every result
