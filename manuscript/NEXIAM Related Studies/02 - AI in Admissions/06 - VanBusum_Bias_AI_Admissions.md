## Relevance to NEXIAM

- **Directly relevant**: evaluates AI model bias specifically in the undergraduate admissions context
- **Key concern**: if NEXIAM's K-Means clustering is used for course triage (aptitude insights), the fairness of such algorithmic grouping must be established
- **NEXIAM alignment**: NEXIAM's human-in-the-loop design (counselor reviews K-Means output before acting on it) directly addresses the bias concern raised by Van Busum & Fang
- **Methodological contrast**: their work evaluates bias in predictive admission models; NEXIAM uses K-Means as a *descriptive* clustering tool (not predictive), which reduces but does not eliminate the need for bias awareness
