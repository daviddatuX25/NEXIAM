## Abstract

> Personal qualities like prosocial purpose and leadership predict important life outcomes, including college success. Unfortunately, the holistic assessment of personal qualities in college admissions is opaque and resource intensive. Can artificial intelligence (AI) advance the goals of holistic assessment? In this research, we developed and validated machine learning models that assess personal qualities from application essays and related activity lists. We showed that models can assess specific personal qualities with adequate accuracy, that the assessments are correlated with life outcomes years later, and that use-inspired fine-tuning can mitigate performance disparities across socioeconomic and demographic subgroups. We discuss opportunities and risks of using AI in college admissions.
