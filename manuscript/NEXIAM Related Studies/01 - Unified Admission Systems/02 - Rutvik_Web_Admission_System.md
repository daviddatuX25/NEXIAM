## Abstract (from Google Scholar snippet)

> In the evolving landscape of higher education, efficient and streamlined admission processes are crucial for both institutions and prospective students. This project presents the development of a web-based college admission system that digitizes the application, processing, and result management workflow.

## Relevance to NEXIAM

- **Directly analogous**: web-based college admission system — exactly the same domain as NEXIAM
- **Feature overlap**: online application intake, digital admission processing
- **NEXIAM distinction**: NEXIAM goes significantly beyond basic web-based admission by integrating computer-vision OMR scoring, K-Means aptitude clustering, RAG chatbot, role-based access control, and RA 10173 privacy governance
- **Level of contribution**: Rutvik et al. describe a basic web-based system; NEXIAM's contribution is the *integrated pipeline* of scoring + profiling + guidance under a governed architecture
