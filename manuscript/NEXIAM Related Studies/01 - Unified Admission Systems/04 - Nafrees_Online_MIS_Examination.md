## Abstract (from Google Scholar snippet)

> There are 16 state universities in Sri Lanka. The main goal of these universities is to develop and strengthen its role as a world-class University through teaching and learning, research and scholarship, and providing highly motivated and skilled graduates with the right attitude. The examination department plays a vital role in maintaining academic standards. This study focuses on the application of an online management information system to streamline examination processes in Sri Lankan state universities.
