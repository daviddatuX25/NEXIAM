## Abstract (from CrossRef)

> Within this study, the authors want to address the problem of overworking of teachers in Philippine schools due to their excessive clerical responsibility, which could lead to teacher burnout. The aim of the study was to determine the effectiveness of automation in evaluating test results using EvalBee as an alternative OMR. The study utilized a quantitative-evaluative approach. Results showed that the automated evaluation using EvalBee significantly reduced the time spent on scoring and provided more accurate results compared to manual scoring. The study recommends the adoption of automated OMR systems in Philippine schools.
