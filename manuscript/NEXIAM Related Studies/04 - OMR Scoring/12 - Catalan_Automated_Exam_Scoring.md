## Abstract (from Google Scholar snippet)

> In this paper, a framework for automatically scoring multiple-choice exams was presented. It has been demonstrated to work in several courses with high accuracy. The use of readily available software makes this framework accessible to institutions with limited resources. Testing was conducted on a total of 800 answer sheets for exams in 8 courses.
