- **Directly relevant**: builds an informed chatbot specifically for university resources — directly analogous to NEXIAM's RAG chatbot for ISPSC policies, procedures, and guidance

- **NEXIAM distinction**: NEXIAM's chatbot is a hybrid RAG system (local embeddings + external LLM generation with privacy sanitization); Neupane et al. focus on building an "informed" chatbot that retrieves from university resource databases
