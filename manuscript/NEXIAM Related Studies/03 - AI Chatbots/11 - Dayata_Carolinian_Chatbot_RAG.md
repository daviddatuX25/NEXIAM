## Relevance to NEXIAM

- **EXTREMELY relevant**: this is a RAG-based chatbot specifically for a Philippine university's (University of San Carlos) policies, procedures, and guidelines — the closest existing implementation to NEXIAM's RAG chatbot for ISPSC
- **Philippine HEI context**: directly comparable — both are Philippine private/state universities implementing RAG for institutional policy Q&A
- **Feature overlap**: RAG pipeline for university policies and procedures — nearly identical to NEXIAM's chatbot design
- **"Carolinian Chatbot"**: named after USC's community identity ("Carolinian"), just as NEXIAM's chatbot serves the ISPSC Tagudin community
- **NEXIAM distinction**: NEXIAM's chatbot adds privacy governance (RA 10173 prompt sanitization), is integrated with the full admission testing pipeline (not standalone), and includes a human-in-the-loop escalation path
- **Key reference for manuscript**: this is the strongest direct precedent to cite for NEXIAM's RAG chatbot feature
