ABSTRACT

YUKEE, AZRIEL JON M., BONIFACIO, CHRISTINE LORENCE., SALVADOR, JOHN MARK A., MACABITAS, ARIES P., A "CLUSTERING STUDENT ICAT SCORE USING MACHINE LEARNING ALGORITHM,"
llocos Sur Polytechnic State College, Tagudin, Ilocos Sur, November 3, 2025. Adviser: GEORGE VILLANUEVA, DIT
The study developed and evaluated a web-based system that utilized a machine
learning clustering algorithm applied to the students' !CAT scores in order to find patterns in academic pe,formance. It was designed to help an institution, through the Guidance office and faculty, to classify students based on competency levels and understand their academic strength and weakness. Following the framework of the CRISP-DM, analysis of existing !CAT score interpretation practices, clustering system development, applying the K-Means algorithm, and assessment of system usability were conducted. The sum result of the archived !CAT data at !locos Sur Polytechnic State College-Tagudin Campus was used/or the analysis. Analysis on the usability test using the System Usability Scale gave a mean score of 68.75 with good ratings on effectiveness, efficiency, and user satisfaction. The K-Means algorithm showed successful determination of optimal clusters into four based on aptitude components, the number of clusters confirmed by the Elbow method, and cluster quality on valuation metrics with a strong meaning. In general, the findings confirm that the system developed in this study is effective in generating meaningful groupings about student performance, supportive of data-driven academic profiling, guidance, placement, and institutional decision-making.
