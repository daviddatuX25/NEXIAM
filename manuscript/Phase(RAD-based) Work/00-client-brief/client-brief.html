This is a compact read-through only. Please correct any item that is incomplete or inaccurate.

Safeguards to confirm

  • ☐ Human confirms OMR results before release.
  • ☐ Manual score entry remains available.
  • ☐ Office can disable student self-booking.
  • ☐ Online consultation follows office policy.
  • ☐ Unauthorized users cannot see confidential exam content.
  • ☐ Counselor remains responsible for advice and placement decisions.
  • ☐ Chatbot can say “I do not have that information” and refer to staff.
  • ☐ Each office sees only information needed for its work.
  • ☐ Paper backup remains available for attendance/outages.
  • ☐ Important record changes are activity-logged.
