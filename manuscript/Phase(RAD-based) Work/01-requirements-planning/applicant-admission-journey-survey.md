# ISPSC TAGUDIN CAMPUS — ADMISSION & TESTING JOURNEY SURVEY
### *(Applicant / First-Year Student Experience & Requirements Validation)*

**Target Respondents:** First-year college students who underwent the recent admission and entrance examination (ICAT) at ISPSC Tagudin Campus.  
**Purpose:** Empirical baseline validation of process pain points (SO1) and requirement justification for digital capabilities (SO2).  
**Estimated Time to Complete:** 5–7 minutes.

---

> **Data Privacy Statement (Republic Act No. 10173 - Data Privacy Act of 2012):**  
> *Ang survey na ito ay bahagi ng undergraduate capstone research para sa pag-aaral at pagpapabuti ng admission at testing process sa ISPSC Tagudin Campus. Ang lahat ng impormasyon at sagot na iyong ibabahagi ay gagamitin lamang para sa layunin ng akademikong pananaliksik at mananatiling kumpidensyal.*
