## Review loop

1. Read the matching section in the researcher-identified defended manuscript.
2. Read the applicable defense notes. Identify requested changes and whether they appear in the defended version; do not assume they were applied.
3. State the section's purpose and each paragraph's job; compare Yukee's presentation without importing its scope or methods.
4. Read the current NEXIAM section. Classify each paragraph as keep, shorten, move, or confirm.
5. Preserve valid approved updates and citations. Place details in the section that owns them.
6. Show only the proposed changes for the current section and obtain researcher approval. Do not apply the whole audit automatically.
7. Edit the canonical Markdown section and record the decision. After publication, check the same content in the browser reader.
8. Continue to the next section only after the current review is settled.

## Narrative format

Use the main section names and order in the approved template and references. Narrative is written in compact paragraphs; a short bold paragraph label may be used where it helps. Do not create nested decimal headings such as `3.3.3` or turn narrative into numbered substeps. Preserve required objective/questionnaire formats, tables, figure/plate captions, and their numbering.

Team instructions such as “confirm the student list” belong in the ledger or clearly separated working metadata, not the submitted prose. Keep unresolved facts visible there; do not replace them with invented completed procedures or numbers. Explain a rationale once in its owning section and cross-reference it where needed.

Long supporting material belongs in appendices, not in the main narrative: full questionnaires in Appendix J; full task procedures and item/coverage maps in Appendix N; use-case, DFD Level 0/1, and ERD material in Appendices C to F. Main chapters retain the method explanation, core requirements/features, key results, and their interpretation.

Use Yukee's presentation pattern, not its limitations or unverified claims. Its full SUS instrument is in Appendix H (reference text lines 469 onward), while Chapter 2 explains the instrument. Existing researcher-provided technical/activity diagrams are selected and placed, not regenerated without a request.
