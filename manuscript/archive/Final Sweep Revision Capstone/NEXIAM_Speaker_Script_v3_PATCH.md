# NEXIAM — Proposal Defense Speaker Script v3
### Patch release against v2 — ONE substantive change, everything else identical
### Change: Slide 5, Click 4 + Defense Anchor, K-Means wording locked to manuscript

---

## What changed and why

v2 was internally consistent with itself but not with the manuscript's
locked K-Means position. The Terminology Record already flagged this as
an open item in its own cross-consistency checklist ("Speaker Script:
Slide 5 Click 4 + Defense Anchor, after B1/B2 script fix") and it was
never actually applied. This patch applies it. No other slide, click, or
timing changes anywhere in the script. Total clicks remain 40, total
estimated time remains ~9 minutes.

If you already have v2 printed or memorized, the only thing to relearn is
the two lines below for Slide 5.

---

## SLIDE 5 — Conceptual Framework (replacement block only)

**Presenter: David. Clicks: 7. (unchanged)**

**[CLICK 4 — Layer 03: K-Means Clustering]**

**OLD (v2):**
> "K-Means clustering groups applicant aptitude patterns descriptively. To
> be precise: it doesn't decide anything for the counselor, it surfaces a
> pattern, the counselor still makes the call."

**NEW (v3):**
> "K-Means clustering groups applicant aptitude patterns into a
> decision-support reference. To be precise: it doesn't decide anything
> for the counselor, it informs the counselor's placement judgment, the
> counselor still makes the call."

**DEFENSE ANCHOR (two — this is the highest-scrutiny slide):**

**OLD (v2):**
- "K-Means is descriptive only, not a recommendation engine. It does not
  decide who gets admitted."
- "AI Scheduling is human-gated. The system proposes, a person approves."

**NEW (v3):**
- "K-Means is a decision-support reference, not a recommendation engine.
  It informs the counselor's placement judgment; it does not decide who
  gets admitted."
- "AI Scheduling is human-gated. The system proposes, a person approves."
  *(unchanged)*

---

## Why this specific wording and not a shorter version

A panelist who has read the manuscript closely will have seen "decision-
support reference" on page 9 and may test whether you can say the same
thing without reaching for "descriptive only," a phrase that no longer
appears anywhere in the manuscript after this patch. Saying "decision-
support reference" verbatim, cold, if interrupted mid-slide, is now the
correct DEFENSE ANCHOR test for this line specifically.
