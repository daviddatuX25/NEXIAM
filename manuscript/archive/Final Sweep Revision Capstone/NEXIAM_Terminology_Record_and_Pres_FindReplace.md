### K-Means Framing -- Locked Position

The locked description across all documents:

> "A decision-support reference that informs the counselor's placement
> judgment rather than replacing it."

The K-Means Aptitude Insights Dashboard surfaces cluster-based aptitude
groupings at the moment of consultation. It is a reference the counselor
actively factors into placement decisions; it does not make the decision
for them. "Descriptive only" is retired from the manuscript. "Does not
decide anything" is retired from the speaker script. The locked framing
above is the single consistent position.

### No Em Dashes

Em dashes (--) do not appear anywhere in the manuscript or speaker
script. Use commas, colons, semicolons, or parentheses instead.
Em dashes in slide text are acceptable because slides are not academic
prose, but the manuscript and speaker script are held to this rule.
