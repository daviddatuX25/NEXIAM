#### 1. Purpose of the Validation Session

This validation protocol establishes official institutional alignment between the machine learning decision-support architecture of NEXIAM and the operational counseling standards of the Guidance and Counseling Office at ISPSC Tagudin Campus. 

Specifically, this session validates the adoption of the in-context tabular foundation model adapter (TabPFN; Hollmann et al., 2025), confirming that algorithmic triage functions strictly as advisory decision support without superseding human counseling authority.
