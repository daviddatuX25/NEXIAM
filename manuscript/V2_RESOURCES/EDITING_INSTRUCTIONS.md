## PART E: Q&A DEFENSE — "But Other Studies Already Did This?"

If a panelist asks about any of the 16 references, use this:

**Q: "Didn't Dayata and Yap already build the chatbot part at USC?"**
> **A**: "Yes — and that's exactly the point. Dayata and Yap proved RAG works for university policy Q&A at the University of San Carlos, published at IEEE COMNETSAT. Cuerdo proved low-cost OMR works in Philippine public schools, published in a peer-reviewed journal. Tan independently validated K-Means for course routing at an ACM conference. Each component is deployment-ready. The gap NEXIAM fills is proving they can all run as one role-governed pipeline under RA 10173 — which no existing study has demonstrated. The integration is the contribution."

**Q: "So Eustaquio and Nisperos already built a registration system at ISPSC — how is NEXIAM different?"**
> **A**: "Eustaquio and Nisperos built a registration and information system with decision-support for billing, grading, and retention — post-admission processes. NEXIAM focuses on the admission testing phase: from applicant registration through scoring, profiling, and counseling support. They don't overlap — Eustaquio picks up after NEXIAM's phase ends. And NEXIAM adds OMR, the chatbot, and K-Means profiling that Eustaquio's system doesn't include."
