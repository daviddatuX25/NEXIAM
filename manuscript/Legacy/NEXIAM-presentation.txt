## Slide 2: The Problem (Global & National)

### A Worldwide Shift, A Familiar Strain

* **Paper-Based Testing Vulnerabilities:** Shifting away due to slow speeds, integrity risks, and equity gaps.
* **Global Shift:** Transitioning to AI-assisted, cloud-based admission platforms where machine learning applicant assessments have been validated *(Lira et al., Science Advances)*.
* **National Strain (RA 10931):** The Free Higher Education Act has significantly increased admission volumes, heavily straining institution staff and infrastructure. Unified platforms have proven successfully deployable with up to 97% usability *(Adwan et al.)*.
* **Legal & Regulatory Floors (RA 10173):** The Data Privacy Act establishes data protection as a mandatory legal duty governing all system designs.

---

## Slide 3: The Problem (At ISPSC Tagudin)

### Same Pressure, Paper Process

* **The Numbers:** **700-1,000 applicants** are processed manually every single year completely on paper.
* **Efficiency Bottlenecks:** Hand-scoring answer sheets creates a delay of five days to two full weeks per cycle.
* **Communication Gaps:** Exam results are posted publicly on a Facebook page, which can only be monitored during office hours. Inter-office coordination completely relies on text messages with no shared system or visibility.
* **The Integration Gap:** Key components have been proven independently but remain unintegrated:
* *RAG Chatbot:* Dayata & Yap (IEEE COMNETSAT)
* *OMR Scoring:* Cuerdo (PH Public Schools)
* *K-Means Routing:* Tan (ACM)
* **Core Issue:** *No unified admission platform currently exists to bring these together.*

## Slide 6: Scope and Limitation

### What NEXIAM Covers - And What It Doesn't

### Scope

* Covers the admission testing workflow only from **June to September 2026**.
* Supports **7 distinct user roles** with full web-based application intake.
* Targeting a **$\ge 95\%$ accuracy rate** for OMR scoring.
* Features active deployment of a RAG chatbot, K-Means aptitude dashboard, and AI-assisted scheduling.

### Limitation

* Excludes enrollment, tuition management, or long-term record keeping.
* The proctoring interface is cloud-connected but maintains offline resilience via service-worker caching for exam venues with intermittent connectivity.
* Free-tier OpenRouter inference caps overall request volume.
* The September evaluation will utilize **simulated evaluators** drawn through purposive sampling, not live applicants.
* No formal NPC certification; data protection is strictly application-layer rather than regulatory-certified.

## Slide 8: Research Design

### Descriptive-Developmental (Frey, 2022)

> **Core Logic:** Diagnose the existing process first, then build and evaluate the solution directly against it.

**Descriptive Phase (SO1 + SO2):**
* Analyze the current manual admission workflow through interviews and field observation
* Extract operational pain points via thematic analysis
* Convert validated gaps into system requirements

**Developmental Phase (SO3 + SO4):**
* Construct NEXIAM through AI-Augmented RAD
* Evaluate user acceptance via paired TAM (pre-test → post-test)
* Test whether NEXIAM significantly outperforms the manual process

## Slide 12: Research Instrument

### Technology Acceptance Model (Davis, 1989)

### Measured Core TAM Constructs:

* **PU:** Perceived Usefulness
* **PEOU:** Perceived Ease of Use
* **ATU:** Attitude Toward Use
* **BIU:** Behavioral Intention to Use

### Execution Details:

* **16 Items Total** role-adapted across four respondent groups (Registrar Personnel, Guidance Personnel, Applicants, Program Heads). The IT Expert is evaluated separately via a structured technical walkthrough outside the TAM instrument.
* Evaluations are scored using a **7-Point Likert Scale** ranging from `1` (Strongly Disagree) to `7` (Strongly Agree).
* **Shift Measurement:** Data is collected twice to measure the actual shift in perception:

$$\text{Pre-Test} \longrightarrow \text{Simulation Exercise} \longrightarrow \text{Post-Test} \longrightarrow \text{Measured Shift in Perception}$$

## Slide 13: Data Analysis Techniques

### Mapped Directly to the Four Specific Objectives

* **SO1 $\rightarrow$ Thematic Analysis:** Surfaces the actual pain-point themes extracted from interview and survey responses.
* **SO2 $\rightarrow$ Iterative Validation:** Proposed system requirements are confirmed with Registrar and Guidance staff directly against real-world workflows.
* **SO3 $\rightarrow$ TAM Means:** Computes pooled and per-role construct means, checking them comprehensively against established acceptance benchmarks.
* **SO4 $\rightarrow$ t-Test & Shapiro-Wilk:** Performs a normality check first, followed by a paired t-test or Wilcoxon signed-rank test at $\alpha = .05$, accompanied by effect size calculations.
