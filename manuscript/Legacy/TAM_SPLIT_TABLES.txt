# NEXIAM — Registrar & Guidance TAM Split (Mirrored + Unique)
# ===================================================================
# Design rule: 2 items mirrored (shared concern), 2 items office-specific
# Mirrored items for PU: visibility (PU-3) and inter-office coordination (PU-4)
# Mirrored items for PEOU: clarity/understandability (PEOU-4) and ease of learning (PEOU-2)
# Mirrored for ATU: satisfaction (ATU-4) and general attitude (ATU-1)
# Mirrored for BIU: overall intention (BIU-4) and recommendation (BIU-2)
# ===================================================================

# SUMMARY: Mirror Pattern

#
# Construct  | Mirrored Items               | Unique Items
# -----------|------------------------------|-----------------------------
# PU         | 3 (visibility) + 4 (coord)   | 1+2 (Reg=eff/speed, Gui=triage)
# PEOU       | 2 (learning) + 4 (clarity)   | 1+3 (Reg=sched/OMR, Gui=triage)
# ATU        | 1 (attitude) + 4 (satisfy)   | 2+3 (Reg=admission, Gui=placement)
# BIU        | 2 (recommend) + 4 (continue) | 1+3 (Reg=cycles, Gui=profiling)
#
# Total: 16 items (4 groups x 4 constructs)
# Groups: Registrar, Guidance, Applicant, Program Head
# IT Expert: outside TAM (technical walkthrough)
# Total evaluators: 47 (46 TAM + 1 IT Expert)
