## 1. Instrument Overview

The Applicant Admission and Testing Journey Survey was administered to evaluate applicant experiences during the manual entrance examination and admission process at ISPSC Tagudin Campus. The survey captured empirical baseline data across application intake, document verification, examination scheduling, counseling queues, and enrollment friction.

* **Target Population:** Enrolled first-year college students and recent examinees who completed the institutional entrance examination.
* **Collection Method:** Online structured questionnaire administered via Google Forms across two collection periods:
  1. *Batch 1 (Cross-Sectional / Senior Cohort):* Administered 28 August 2026 ($n = 12$) across BS Information Technology, BA Psychology, BA English Language, BS Mathematics, and BS Business Administration.
  2. *Batch 2 (Freshman Class Cohort):* Administered 29 September 2026 ($n = 10$) among enrolled first-year College of Business Management and Entrepreneurship (CBME) students.
* **Total Sample Size:** $n = 22$ respondents.

| Physical Queue Wait Duration | Frequency (f) | Percentage (%) |
|---|:---:|:---:|
