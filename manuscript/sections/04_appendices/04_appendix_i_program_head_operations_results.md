## 1. Instrument Overview

The Academic Program Head Enrollment Operations Survey was administered on 25 June 2026 during the Requirements Planning phase to evaluate how departmental quotas, course prerequisites, and applicant referral endorsements are managed between academic departments, the Guidance Office, and the Registrar Office.

* **Target Population:** Academic Program Heads and Department Chairs representing undergraduate degree programs at ISPSC Tagudin Campus.
* **Collection Method:** Structured operational questionnaire evaluating communication methods, update frequencies, curriculum planning bottlenecks, and feature ratings for proposed digital coordination dashboards.

---

## 2. Empirical Survey Findings and Departmental Baseline

### Section 1: Quota Communication Channels and Update Frequencies
* **Primary Communication Method:** Departmental quotas and remaining slot statuses are communicated to the Guidance Office through **face-to-face meetings**.
* **Update Frequency:** The respondent reported **daily updates**, especially during the admission period.
* **Coordination Difficulty:** Program Heads noted that tracking incoming student volumes manually affects departmental preparedness for section scheduling, faculty room assignments, and instructional material procurement.

The survey probed the utility of proposed digital coordination capabilities on a three-level scale (Highly Useful, Moderately Useful, Not Useful):

### Section 3: Qualitative Feedback and Feature Requests
* **Direct Feedback Quote:** *"A feature that can contact directly the concerned persons, for example, regarding follow-ups, special endorsements, or student appeals without relying on informal phone calls."*
* **Architectural Fulfillment:** This field request directly justified the implementation of the **Scoped Department Access Interface and Bound Messaging Engine (FR-13)**, allowing Program Heads and Deans to communicate endorsement decisions and track student appeals within an authenticated audit trail.
