# Chapter 1: Introduction

## 1.4 Scope and Limitations of the Study

This study covers the design, development, and evaluation of NEXIAM for the Guidance and Registrar Offices at Ilocos Sur Polytechnic State College (ISPSC) Tagudin Campus during the June to September 2026 admission cycle. It focuses on the admission-testing process, from applicant intake and examination scheduling through testing, score preparation, result release, guidance consultation, and Registrar review of enrollment eligibility.

System operations are structured around Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across distinct operational groups: applicants, Guidance personnel (including the Guidance Counselor / Test Administrator and support staff), Registrar personnel, proctors assigned for specific examination sessions, and Academic Program Heads or Deans who access scoped departmental dashboards for quota tracking and referral endorsements. An emergency Super Administrator capability is maintained strictly for recovery and is excluded from user evaluation.

For admission decision support, the system evaluates applicant aptitude across the six cognitive areas measured by the Ilocos Sur Polytechnic State College College Admission Test (ICAT): General Ability, Verbal Aptitude, Numerical Aptitude, Spatial Aptitude, Perceptual Aptitude, and Manual Dexterity, alongside Senior High School academic background (strand and Grade 12 General Weighted Average). Decision support is implemented through a pre-trained tabular foundation model adapter (TabPFN; Hollmann et al., 2025) executing in-context inference against college-approved benchmark exemplars to provide counselors with advisory course compatibility percentages and primary feature drivers. The platform operates strictly as an advisory tool: it holds no autonomous authority to admit students, set quotas, or confirm enrollments. All course recommendations and status changes require explicit confirmation by the Guidance Counselor, while department heads manage quotas separately.

The study is bounded to the admission-testing workflow at ISPSC Tagudin Campus and does not encompass other campuses, fee assessment, actual enrollment registration, transcript generation, or student disciplinary archives. The investigation does not evaluate longitudinal predictive validity (such as multi-year retention or board examination outcomes), nor does it benchmark the TabPFN model against external machine learning baselines. Non-cognitive factors such as personality traits and socioeconomic status are not evaluated.

NEXIAM operates as a web-based system requiring active internet connectivity for routine administrative workflows. Offline operational resilience is provided exclusively for the examination hall proctoring interface via a local outbox and signed session tokens to accommodate intermittent connectivity. Formal certification from the National Privacy Commission (NPC) and a third-party Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) fall outside the scope of this study, with data privacy addressed through RBAC isolation, input validation, and an HMAC-SHA256 cryptographically chained audit log.

User acceptance is evaluated through a single post-test using the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) across a five-point Likert scale, measuring Perceived Usefulness and Perceived Ease of Use. The planned sample comprises 263 evaluators across four respondent groups: Registrar Personnel (n = 3, complete census), Guidance Personnel (n = 2, complete census), Academic Program Heads or Deans (n = 3, purposive selection of one representative from each department: CAS, CBME, and CTE), and First-Year College Students (current target n = 255). The student sample calculation and selection procedure are addressed in Chapter 2.

The user-acceptance evaluation follows staff-managed examination scheduling and face-to-face consultation with result-sheet delivery. Researchers prepare the answer-sheet design, scoring configuration, and institutional reference data before the sessions. Staff evaluate their use of the prepared scoring function, not answer-sheet building or mark-configuration validation. Applicant slot selection, online consultation, and separate online result release are outside this evaluation. These exclusions describe the evaluation boundary, not the usefulness or acceptance of those optional functions. Office coordination is evaluated through the approved staff communication tasks.
