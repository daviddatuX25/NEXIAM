# Chapter 1: Introduction

## 1.5 Importance of the Study

The development and implementation of NEXIAM provide practical and institutional benefits to the operational units, academic administrators, and student stakeholders of Ilocos Sur Polytechnic State College (ISPSC) Tagudin Campus, while contributing a reference architecture to educational technology research.

### Guidance Office Personnel
For Guidance Counselors and examination administrators, the platform eliminates the labor-intensive burden of manual stencil checking. Automating answer-sheet evaluation through computer vision compresses the scoring cycle from more than five days to mere minutes, preventing staff exhaustion and eliminating transcription errors. Furthermore, the attributable consultation workbench replaces undocumented oral advising with persistent digital records. The integration of TabPFN in-context course triage provides objective, calibrated aptitude indicators and primary feature drivers, assisting counselors during compressed interview windows without compromising human guidance authority.

### Registrar Office Personnel
For Registrar administrators and staff, the system replaces physical paper folders and disconnected spreadsheets with a unified digital intake queue. Staff can review application requirements, request document corrections with explicit audit trails, and verify applicant eligibility in real time. The automated enrollment eligibility engine computes student clearance derived from verified application, completed testing, released scores, and completed counseling, eliminating cross-office physical document handoffs and reducing administrative reconciliation delays during enrollment.

### Academic Program Heads and Deans
For department heads, academic deans, and curriculum planners, the platform delivers real-time visibility into departmental program quotas and pre-enrollment rosters. By replacing informal verbal quota inquiries with live capacity utilization bars, Program Heads and Deans can anticipate incoming student cohorts, manage class sections proactively, and prevent over-enrollment in high-demand academic disciplines. The aggregate triage indicators provide empirical evidence regarding incoming student aptitude distributions, informing departmental curriculum development.

### Prospective Student Applicants
For incoming college applicants, NEXIAM transforms a fragmented, stressful admission journey into a transparent, self-service experience. The mobile-responsive portal enables applicants to track admission milestones, verify document acceptance, check examination assignments, and view official test results securely from home. By eliminating the necessity of three physical campus visits, the platform removes substantial transportation expenses for the sixty percent of applicants traveling from outlying provincial municipalities, supporting educational equity under Republic Act No. 10931.

### Institutional Administration and Data Protection Officers
For college executives and institutional planners, the platform establishes a persistent, auditable system of record for college admissions. By incorporating Role-Based Access Control and an HMAC-SHA256 cryptographically chained audit log, NEXIAM operationalizes compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). The resulting database provides a reliable institutional research asset, enabling long-term longitudinal studies on applicant admission profiles, counseling interventions, and subsequent college retention.

### Future Researchers and Educational Technologists
For researchers in applied artificial intelligence and educational informatics, this study provides an empirical case study in adopting tabular foundation models within resource-constrained higher education institutions. It demonstrates how in-context learning through TabPFN overcomes the small-sample overfitting dilemma inherent in small state college cohorts, offering a reproducible framework for deploying human-in-the-loop decision support without requiring expensive local model retraining.
