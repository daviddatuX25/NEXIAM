# Chapter 1: Introduction

## 1.3 Objectives of the Study

The general objective of this study is to develop and evaluate NEXIAM: A Role-Based College Admission Testing System for the Guidance and Registrar Offices at Ilocos Sur Polytechnic State College (ISPSC) Tagudin Campus. The web-based platform centralizes and digitizes the admission testing pipeline covering applicant intake, examination scheduling, proctoring, computer-vision scoring, decision support, result delivery, and enrollment eligibility.

More specifically, this study seeks to accomplish the following specific objectives:

1. **Specific Objective 1 (Process Analysis):** To analyze the existing college admission testing processes, operational workflows, and process gaps at ISPSC Tagudin Campus through descriptive inquiry, identifying specific failure points across administrative offices and applicant journeys.

2. **Specific Objective 2 (System Requirements and Software Development):** To determine the system requirements of NEXIAM in terms of the functional requirements and system features needed to address the identified process gaps.

3. **Specific Objective 3 (Acceptance Evaluation):** To evaluate the acceptance of NEXIAM using the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) in terms of Perceived Usefulness (PU) and Perceived Ease of Use (PEOU) across four respondent groups: Registrar Personnel, Guidance Personnel, First-Year College Students, and Academic Program Heads or Deans.
