# Canonical Domain Glossary Extract

*This document is a portable, verbatim extract of NEXIAM's root `GLOSSARY.md`. It provides researchers working outside the development monorepo with the official domain definitions governing testing, psychometrics, examination operations, and institutional clearance.*

---

## 1. Assessment & Psychometrics

### Test Instrument
An identified assessment with an edition or form, administration instructions, scoring rules, and intended interpretation. An instrument is not an answer-sheet layout or a conversion table.

### Test Section / Subtest
A named part of a test instrument, such as Vocabulary or Computation, with its own items or measurement procedure. A subtest is distinct from an aptitude construct and may occupy several answer-sheet blocks or pages.
- *Avoid:* "Domain one", "aptitude area" when referring to a physical test's item group.

### Answer-Sheet Mapping Block
A group of answer locations on a particular sheet page. Several blocks may represent items belonging to one subtest.
- *Avoid:* "Subtest" when referring only to a group of plotted bubbles.

### Aptitude Construct
The ability being assessed, such as Verbal Aptitude or General Ability. A construct is distinct from the subtests or measurements used to assess it.

### Item Observation
The selected response, confirmed absence of a response, or unresolved mark recorded for an item. An observation does not itself establish whether the answer is correct.

### Item Score
The points awarded to an item under the applicable scoring rule and answer key. An item score is distinct from a norm-converted result.

### Raw Metric
A result before norm conversion, obtained from item scores, an approved measurement procedure, or a defined combination of other raw metrics. Its units and validity belong to the test instrument.

### Derived Raw Metric
A raw metric obtained from specified component raw metrics rather than a separate set of answered items. A composite does not imply an independent answer-sheet section.

### Norm Conversion
The interpretation of an applicable raw metric using an approved reference population and conversion table. A raw percentage is not necessarily a percentile rank.

### Norm Package
An identified set of norm tables with its source, edition, applicable population, and output measures. A package name alone does not establish applicability or approval.

### Percentile Rank
A norm-relative measure of position in a specified reference population. It is not the fraction of test items answered correctly.

### Stanine
A nine-category norm-referenced score (1 to 9). It is not interchangeable with a raw score or percentile rank.

## 2. Examination Operations

### Simple-Capacity Exam
An exam scheduled by the number of applicants a room can accommodate, without prescribing each applicant's physical seat.

### Assigned-Seating Exam
An exam whose published plan assigns applicants to specific physical seats. Its seating plan remains part of that exam's recorded administration.

### Booklet Set
A particular ordered form of one Booklet with its own corresponding answer key. One Booklet normally uses one Set; different Booklets do not share a key merely because each uses a single Set.

### Booklet Distribution List
A session-specific list telling authorized exam staff which Booklet Set to give each applicant. It does not contain correct answers.

## 3. Admission Clearance & Handoff

### Admission Clearance
Registrar's confirmation that an applicant has satisfied the admission requirements for the specified program and can proceed to the institution's separate enrollment process. It does not confirm that the applicant is enrolled.
- *Avoid:* "Enrolling", "enrolled", "final enrollment" when describing admission clearance.

### Enrollment-Sheet Handoff
The recorded physical issue of an enrollment sheet to an applicant after admission clearance. It does not prove that the external enrollment process has started or finished.

### Paper Follow-Up Verification
An authorized staff member's verification of an academic follow-up outcome recorded on institutional paper. It is distinct from a Program Head or Dean recording that outcome digitally.
