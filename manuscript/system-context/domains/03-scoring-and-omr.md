# Domain 03: Assessment Scoring & Computer Vision OMR

## 1. Domain Responsibility & Academic Value

The Assessment Scoring & Computer Vision Optical Mark Recognition (OMR) domain automates the capture, recognition, verification, and psychometric conversion of paper answer sheets for the ICAT examination.

In the capstone research, this domain directly addresses primary problem statement **FM1** (High turnaround time, labor bottlenecks, and transcription risks in manually checking 1,000+ paper answer sheets) documented in the Guidance Office baseline validation.

---

## 2. Dual-Mode OMR Architecture

1. **`system_designed` (Visual Designer):**
   - Synthesizes print-ready PDF/SVG answer sheets with corner fiducial markers.
   - Enforces a minimum 4.2 mm bubble diameter and 1.26 mm option pitch for optimal consumer webcam recognition.
2. **`uploaded_original` (Legacy & Supplier Sheets):**
   - Ingests arbitrary pre-printed sheets (e.g., historical OASIS-3 sheets with 2.84–3.39 mm bubbles).
   - Features a **2.5 mm minimum bubble floor** (`minimum_uploaded_bubble_mm = 2.5`).
   - Supports arbitrary option tokens: paired comparisons (`["A-B", "A-C", "A-D"]`), True/False, and custom codes up to 32 characters.

## 3. Computer Vision Processing Pipeline

The CV pipeline operates within an isolated Docker service (`system/docker/inference/`):

1. **Corner Detection & Homography Rectification (`document_rectifier.py`):**
   - Ingests raw photographic captures (mobile camera, flatbed scanner).
   - Locates document boundary corners using contour quad-fitting and applies a perspective warp to normalize tilt, rotation, and skew.
   - Multi-page sheets are processed sequentially (`Page 1 of 2` $\rightarrow$ `Page 2 of 2`).
2. **Bubble Density Analysis (`OmrCheckerDetection.php`):**
   - Analyzes pixel saturation across bounding boxes mapped in `NativeMappingBlock`.
   - Emits an array of `OmrDetectedItem`: bounding box, recognized value, and numeric confidence.

---

## 4. Uncertainty Handling: The Human-in-the-Loop Review Desk

NEXIAM rejects fully autonomous grading without oversight. An item is routed to the **`OmrItemReview` desk** when:
- The fill ratio is borderline (light shading or faint erasure).
- Multiple bubbles are shaded for a single-choice question.
- The applicant number or booklet set barcode cannot be resolved.

**Test Administrator Workflow:**
- The staff member views the magnified high-resolution optical snippet alongside the model's proposal.
- The administrator confirms or overrides the value; the action is committed to an append-only audit log (`OmrItemReviewRevision`).

---

## 5. Answer Keys & Psychometric Norm Conversion

Once optical observations are validated:

1. **Key Application:** `EvaluateItemResponses` checks clean responses against the active `OmrQuestionBookletVersion` for the examinee's assigned Booklet Set (default **Set A**).
2. **Raw Metric Computation:** Scores are totaled by subtest (e.g., Mathematics = 32/40; English = 28/40).
3. **Norm Package Translation (`NormPackage`):** Raw metrics are translated into:
   - **Stanine Scores (1 to 9):** Standardized nine-band metric used for departmental qualification.
   - **Percentile Ranks (1 to 99):** Relative institutional standing.
