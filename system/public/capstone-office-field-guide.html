🧠 1. GUIDANCE OFFICE (Testing & Counseling)

Target audience: Guidance Director, Testing Coordinators, Counselors.

What to Present

  • The Consultation Dashboard (/consultation): Today's scheduled sessions, room capacities, and list of applicants pending advising.
  • Click-to-Pool Advising: Click once to add a program; the first clicked becomes the Advised Default. Click again to remove. Zero typing.
  • Admission Support Models (Decision Support): Side-by-side department criteria checklist + Aptitude Profile Radar Chart. Emphasize: "Models advise — you decide."
  • The Physical Result Sheet: Shows the printable Result Sheet with opaque QR pass that students hand-carry to the Registrar.

Specific Questions to Ask Guidance

  • During busy advising days, does a click-to-select program pool (with the first click as the recommended default) feel fast and natural?
  • Would having a side-by-side checklist showing department cutoffs (e.g. Math ≥ 80) and live remaining seats help you advise borderline students?
  • When an applicant misses a cutoff by 1–2 points, do you currently refer them to the Dean/Program Head, or does Guidance handle the exception?
  • Would you prefer scheduling hourly consultation batches (e.g., 20/hour) over open morning walk-in crowds?

Counselor Pitch (Taglish): “Ma’am, sa screen po na ito, hindi niyo na kailangang mag-type ng mahabang forms habang may nakapilang estudyante. Pag-upo ng bata, kita niyo agad ang score radar at department cutoffs. Click niyo lang po ang kurso—yung unang click, yun na po ang recommended default na hahawak ng slot niya. Kapag tapos na po, print lang ng Result Sheet na may QR code, at diretso na ang bata sa Registrar.”

🏛️ 2. REGISTRAR OFFICE (Admissions & Enrollment Verification)

Target audience: University Registrar, Admissions Clerks, Counter Evaluators.

What to Present

  • 2-Second Counter Check: Zero re-encoding. Registrar scans QR or types name and immediately verifies:
    ✓ DELA CRUZ, JUAN A. (APP-2026-0042) STATUS: CLEARED FOR ENROLLMENT Advised Track: BS Information Technology
  • "Signed Paper is Final Truth": If the physical paper says BSCS instead of BSIT, the system enrolls BSCS, releases the old BSIT seat, and logs the change as research data.
  • Post-Enrollment SIS Masterlist Sync: Registrar never has to encode student-by-student; can upload an official SIS CSV after enrollment week.

Specific Questions to Ask the Registrar

  • On enrollment day, is seeing a fast "CLEARED FOR ENROLLMENT" status the primary verification you need at your counter?
  • If a student presents an official printed Result Sheet signed by Guidance, is that physical paper sufficient, or is a digital checkmark required?
  • During peak enrollment rush, would you prefer NOT having to encode the final course in our software, and instead provide the official enrolled list after enrollment closes?
  • When a student brings an authorized waiver to enroll in a course already at full capacity, how does your office currently record that override?

Registrar Pitch (Taglish): “Sir/Ma’am, naiintindihan po namin na sa araw ng enrollment, tambak kayo ng trabaho sa assessment at sectioning. Ang role po ng NEXIAM sa desk niyo ay 2-second clearance lang: scan or search, makikita niyo agad na tapos ang exam at counseling, ‘Cleared to Enroll’. Hindi po kayo pipigilan ng software kung may piradong waiver ang bata. At pagkatapos ng enrollment week, pwede niyo pong i-upload ang official masterlist para ma-match ng system ang analytics.”

🎓 3. PROGRAM HEADS & DEANS (Department Review & Quotas)

Target audience: Department Chairs, College Deans, Program Coordinators.

What to Present

  • Zero Bottlenecks: Normal, qualified applicants bypass Program Heads completely. 90% of students enroll without waiting for departmental clicks.
  • Clean Follow-Up Queue: Program Heads only see students referred by Guidance (e.g., bridging candidates, interview requirements, quota appeals).
  • Direct Mobile/Desktop Actions: Three simple choices:
    • [ ✓ Clear / Approve ]
    • [ ✕ Decline ] (releases quota slot)
    • [ 📅 Request Office Interview ]
  • Departmental Quota Tracker: Live view of seat capacity (e.g. 35 / 50 seats filled).

Specific Questions to Ask Program Heads

  • For normal applicants who meet all published cutoffs, do you agree that Guidance should advise them directly without requiring your signature?
  • When Guidance refers borderline applicants to your department, would you prefer a simple digital queue on your phone/desktop, or physical referral slips?
  • For quota-limited programs (e.g. Nursing, Tech), who holds the final institutional authority to adjust or override the student cap?

📝 4. POST-ADMISSION SURVEY (Institutional Research Engine)

Value-Add for Academic Planning: Once marked ENROLLED, students see an optional 1-minute survey. For students whose enrolled program differs from their advised program, a conditional question unlocks:

"We noticed you enrolled in BS Computer Science (BSCS) after being advised for BS Information Technology (BSIT). What was the primary reason? ( ) Quota / Slots Full in First Choice ( ) Family / Parental Preference ( ) Career Opportunity Realization ( ) Changed Mind After Counseling"

Institutional Payoff: Provides the University President, Deans, and Guidance Director with hard statistical data showing why students shift courses, proving where new faculty and classroom sections are needed for the next school year.
