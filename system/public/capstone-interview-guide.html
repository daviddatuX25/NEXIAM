Meeting outcome

Leave with confirmed process wording, decision owners, booking rules, OMR exception rules, release rules, and annotated wireframe corrections. Do not change production screens during the interview.

Interview sequence

01 · Align

System vision

Confirm that the office wants one visible record from application through result release.

02 · Walk

Current process

Ask the client to narrate the real handoffs, owners, exceptions, and paper/manual work.

03 · Validate

Future process

Review the decision matrix and mark each row confirmed, changed, or still open.

04 · Review

Interfaces

Walk the wireframes and confirm dashboards, cards, statistics, lists, detail pages, actions, and states.

Briefer script · Taglish

Opening: “Ma’am/Sir, mabilis na walkthrough lang po. Ipapakita namin ang current process, proposed feature, at flow. Please correct us kapag may mali.”

For each row: “Ito po ang current way. Ito ang proposed change. Agree po ba, modify, keep current, or defer?”

For the evidence: “Ito ang na-record nating finding from the earlier interview. Tama po ba ito sa actual na office process?”

For an open rule: “Hindi muna namin ito ita-treat na final. Ano po ang exact rule, owner, and follow-up?”

Closing: “Ire-record namin ang corrections, decisions, and open questions. Hindi pa ito production approval unless marked or stated by the office.”

Questions to ask

  1. Which three handoffs cause the most follow-up today?
  2. What must Guidance see first when opening an applicant?
  3. Which changes require Registrar, Guidance, or Program Head approval?
  4. Which examination and consultation activities may an applicant book?
  5. What should happen when an OMR sheet is unreadable, disputed, or incomplete?
  6. What must be written in a follow-up remark before a case can close?
  7. What must be complete before a result can be released, printed, or reversed?
  8. Which screen labels, dashboard cards, statistics, or table columns are missing?
