Feature evidence to explain

Legacy adaptation

Admissions, applicant access, consultation, examinations, grading, release, and setup are being re-adopted with visible ownership, status, authority, and audit evidence.

Flagship additions

Nexi AI, the chatbot/assistant experience, OMR-powered checking, and the OMR template builder extend the legacy workflow instead of being hidden as isolated demos.

Interface completeness

Each feature is checked against its dashboard, cards/statistics, list/table, detail/workspace, action controls, empty/loading/error states, and audit/history needs.

Still requiring client confirmation

  • Booking rules for examination and consultation.
  • OMR exception and manual fallback rules.
  • Follow-up fields, owners, and closure conditions.
  • Authority required for correction, quota, release, and reversal.
  • Office vocabulary and missing interface inclusions.
