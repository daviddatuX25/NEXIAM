#### PART II: EVALUATION INSTRUCTIONS AND RATING BENCHMARK

Rate only the functions or result sheets you used during the assigned tasks. Check one response per statement. Answer only the questionnaire for your assigned role.

* **5: Strongly Agree (SA)**
* **4: Agree (A)**
* **3: Neutral (N)**
* **2: Disagree (D)**
* **1: Strongly Disagree (SD)**

Neutral means neither agreement nor disagreement. If a function was not available, not permitted, or not tested, tell the facilitator and leave its item unanswered. If you tried a function but could not complete the task, you may rate it based on that experience. Do not use Neutral to report an untested function. Task problems and help received are recorded separately.
